Why this is the right fit for tastytrade
You're already looking at Optimizely for web experimentation.

Amplitude Web Experiment gives you the same capability — visual editor, no-code variant testing, statistical significance — but experiments run against the same behavioral data already in your platform. No duplicate instrumentation, no extra vendor to manage, and your winning variants are tied directly to funded account conversion, not just surface-level metrics.

You're regulated.

RBAC and data access controls aren't a nice-to-have — they're required before your compliance team will sign off.

Your traders notice everything.

Hourly alerts catch anomalies before they become support calls. Daily alerting isn't fast enough.

Your engineers need staging.

No one on your tech team will instrument production without a QA environment to test against first.

You have enough user segments to need 20 predictions.

Risk profile, account type, activity tier, product preference — Growth's 3 won't cut it.

Experiment integrity matters.

Testing onboarding paths for options vs. equity traders requires holdout groups — Growth doesn't have them.

You've built something worth protecting.

Your data is clean and trusted. Enterprise gives you the governance to keep it that way as you open access.
