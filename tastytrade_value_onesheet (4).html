Funded account → first trade conversion

Your most important and most broken funnel
Product analytics

The problem you described

  • !
    Users apply, get approved, fund an account — then disappear without trading. You've done all the hard work and they still leave.
  • !
    Your funnel has multiple milestones (apply → approve → fund → trade) but only the last two generate revenue.
  • !
    You get good KYC data on risk profile but haven't been able to use it to personalize the onboarding path.
  • !
    Left-side-of-curve customers (less sophisticated traders) are landing and churning because the product wasn't built for them — but they're coming through the door.

What Amplitude would do

  • →
    Build a multi-step behavioral funnel across the full application → approval → funding → first trade path with precise drop-off visibility
  • →
    Use session replay auto-linked to drop-off events — watch exactly what funded-but-never-traded users did in their last session
  • →
    Ask the AI agent: "Which behaviors in the first 48 hours after funding most predict placing a first trade?" — get a predictive model, not a hunch
  • →
    Tag users with KYC-derived properties (risk profile, account type) and build personalized onboarding guides — stocks path vs. options path vs. futures path
Key question answered: Why are funded accounts not trading, and what do we do about it?

Product analytics

The problem you described

  • !
    Traders fund an account, make a few losing trades, and leave. You don't have an early warning system.
  • !
    Active users going weeks or months without logging in — you're not catching the drift.
  • !

  • →
    Build predictive churn cohorts — Amplitude identifies the behavioral signals (withdrawal initiated, declining session frequency, no feature engagement) that precede churning
  • →

    Trigger in-product guides or campaigns when at-risk signals fire — a nudge at the right moment, not a generic email blast three weeks too late

Application funnel optimization

Fix the 8-10 page application before you lose them
Product analytics

The problem you described

  • !
    Your application is 8–10 pages. You don't know exactly where people are abandoning — or why.
  • !
    The tension between too many pages vs. too many questions on one page is a real UX tradeoff you're navigating blind today.
  • !
    Users who start the application but don't complete it represent your highest-intent, lowest-friction acquisition opportunity — and you're losing them without knowing where.
  • !
    Adding a page feels like a risk. Removing questions feels like a compliance issue. Right now there's no data to arbitrate that debate.

What Amplitude would do

  • →
    Build a step-by-step funnel across every page of the application — see exactly which page, which field, which moment causes drop-off
  • →
    Session replay auto-linked to abandonment events — watch what users actually do on the pages where you lose them most
  • →
    A/B test page structure vs. field consolidation — get a statistically significant answer to the one-question-per-page debate instead of guessing
  • →
    Identify which applicant profiles complete at higher rates — use KYC signals to personalize the flow for low-risk vs. high-risk applicants from page one
Key question answered: Where exactly are we losing applicants, and what do we change?
