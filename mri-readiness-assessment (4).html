MRI just launched Agora Intelligence and Agora Orchestrator — AI products designed to surface recommendations and automate workflows for real estate teams. That's a significant bet on AI-powered product experiences.

But campaign attribution is still a black box. Your website is generating behavior data nobody is reading. And every time ROI comes up internally, someone has a different number. Amplitude helps you solve that — and measure whether your own AI products are actually working for users.

Why even do this — four problems worth solving
1
Stop guessing where marketing budget should go

Without a clear line from campaign to closed deal, spend gets allocated on instinct. Unifying behavioral and account data across your CRM instances shows the real path from first touch to revenue — so budget shifts toward what's working.

Impact: better allocated marketing spend
2
Find out why engaged prospects never convert

You have prospects who attend webinars, download content, take intro calls — and never open as an opportunity. Amplitude lets you build cohorts of those users, find the commonalities, and run campaigns specifically designed to move them. Right now that analysis doesn't exist.

Impact: unlock stalled pipeline
3
See where your website is losing deals before they get to sales

You're running UTMs now, but still working with Google Analytics. Website behavioral data shows exactly where prospects drop off before filling out a form — and which pages actually drive pipeline. No product team involvement required.

Impact: better conversion, faster pipeline
4
Replace the attribution debate with an answer

Every attribution conversation turns into a different model and a different opinion. One consistent source of truth — stitching together your campaigns, website behavior, and CRM data — means the team acts on the answer instead of arguing about it.

Impact: faster, more confident decisions

What a phased start looks like for MRI
Crawl
Website behavior — no CRM required

Deploy on your marketing site and start capturing visitor behavior — pages viewed, drop-off points, clicks. No CRM integration needed.

What you need: GTM access or a developer for SDK install.

Walk
Connect Salesforce 360 — attribution starts here

Once Flashlight / Salesforce 360 consolidation is further along, connect that master account ID to Amplitude and tie website behavior to real accounts.

What you need: A stable master account ID from Salesforce 360 or Data Cloud.

Run
Full funnel — campaign to product to revenue

Web behavior, CRM data, and product usage in one view. Attribution models, cohort analysis, audience syncs back to Pardot and LinkedIn.

What you need: Data warehouse connected, product instrumentation in place.
