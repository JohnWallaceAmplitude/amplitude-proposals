Why Enterprise is the right fit for tastytrade

You're regulated. RBAC and data access controls aren't a nice-to-have — they're required before your compliance team will sign off.
Your traders notice everything. Hourly alerts catch anomalies before they become support calls. Daily alerting isn't fast enough.
Your engineers need staging. No one on your tech team will instrument production without a QA environment to test against first.
You have enough user segments to need 20 predictions. Risk profile, account type, activity tier, product preference — Growth's 3 won't cut it.
Experiment integrity matters. Testing onboarding paths for options vs. equity traders requires holdout groups — Growth doesn't have them.
You've built something worth protecting. Your data is clean and trusted. Enterprise gives you the governance to keep it that way as you open access.
