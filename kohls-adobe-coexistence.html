The Case for Both

Adobe does what Adobe does.
Amplitude fills what it doesn't.

Adobe Analytics and Target are built for marketing measurement and campaign optimization. Amplitude operates in a different layer entirely — behavioral product intelligence, session-level visibility, and engineering-speed experimentation. These tools don't compete. They cover different ground.

Where Amplitude Adds Value at Kohl's
Session Replay — Immediate Value
Watch AI-analyzed recordings of user sessions across Kohl's digital properties. Identify exactly where users rage click, drop off checkout flows, or abandon search. Adobe has no answer here.
Feature Experimentation for Engineering
Engineering teams run feature flags and in-product A/B tests independently of Adobe Target. Ship faster, roll back safely, and measure impact at the user behavior level — not just the page level.
Product Funnel & Retention Analysis
Understand where users drop off between browsing and purchase, which cohorts have higher LTV, and what drives repeat visits — user-level analysis Adobe Analytics wasn't built for.
Cross-Channel Identity
Stitch together the anonymous shopper on mobile with the authenticated account on desktop. See the complete journey — not fragments across sessions and devices.

The recommendation isn't Adobe or Amplitude — it's Adobe and Amplitude.
Adobe owns marketing measurement and campaign optimization. Amplitude owns behavioral product intelligence, session visibility, and engineering-speed experimentation. The two tools operate in different layers of the stack and are deployed together by some of the largest retailers in the world.
